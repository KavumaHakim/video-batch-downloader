FFmpeg bundled with Video Batch Downloader
==========================================

This application ships with FFmpeg so that audio conversion, stream merging
and subtitle handling work without a separate download. FFmpeg is a separate
program, written by the FFmpeg developers, not by this project. The app runs
it as an external process; no FFmpeg code is linked into the application.

What is included
----------------
ffmpeg.exe and ffprobe.exe from the "release-essentials" Windows build
published by Gyan Doshi at https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/, which is the
Windows build linked from the official https://ffmpeg.org/download.html page.

These binaries are configured with --enable-gpl --enable-version3 and are
therefore covered by the GNU General Public License, version 3 or later.
The full licence text is in LICENSE.txt beside this file, and the build's own
notes, including the exact component licences, are in README.txt.

Getting the source
------------------
The complete corresponding source for these binaries is available, free of
charge, from the same place the binaries come from:

  https://www.gyan.dev/ffmpeg/builds/        (source archives are linked there)
  https://ffmpeg.org/download.html           (upstream releases)
  https://git.ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg.git          (upstream source repository)

If you would rather receive the source from us, open an issue at
https://github.com/KavumaHakim/video-batch-downloader/issues and we will
provide it.

Your rights
-----------
The GPL gives you the right to use, study, share and modify FFmpeg. Nothing
about bundling it here limits those rights. You may delete the ffmpeg folder
and point the app at your own FFmpeg build instead: Settings has an FFmpeg
path field for exactly that, and the app will use whatever you put there in
preference to the bundled copy.

Video Batch Downloader itself is a separate work that merely invokes FFmpeg,
and is distributed under its own licence.
